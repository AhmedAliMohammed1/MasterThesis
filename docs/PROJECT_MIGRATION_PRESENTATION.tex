\begin{frame}[t]{Why we updated the original code}
\small\raggedright
\textbf{Project purpose:} This project uses PointPillars to detect vehicles,
pedestrians and cyclists in LiDAR point clouds, then publishes their 3D boxes
through ROS 2 for visualization and use by other applications.

\smallskip
\centering\resizebox{.96\linewidth}{!}{\migrationfigure}\par
\raggedright
\small
Old ROS fields and TensorRT APIs did not fit the new stack. We also needed
checked data sizes, reliable cleanup and fewer GPU allocations.

\medskip
\begingroup\footnotesize
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{0.39\linewidth}Y@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Main files we changed or added} & \textbf{What the change gives us} \\
\midrule
\file{CMakeLists.txt},\newline \file{package.xml} & C++17, explicit dependencies and CPU tests without a GPU. \\
\file{pp_inference_node.cpp},\newline \file{pointcloud_adapter.cpp} & Jazzy messages and checks before accepting a cloud. \\
\file{pointpillar.cpp},\newline \file{pointpillar.h} & TensorRT 10 named tensors, checked results and persistent runtime. \\
\file{cuda_resources.hpp},\newline \file{output_contract.hpp} & Reused GPU buffers, automatic cleanup and valid output counts. \\
\file{postprocess.cpp},\newline \file{postprocess.h} & Tested rotated box overlap and duplicate removal (NMS). \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\endgroup

\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[t]{From ONNX to a running ROS node}
\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
\begin{column}{0.49\textwidth}
\centering
\resizebox{!}{76mm}{\enginefigure}
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.48\textwidth}
\small\raggedright
\textbf{Two files, two jobs}

\file{.onnx}: trained network and weights, read by the builder.
\file{.engine}: GPU execution plan, read by the ROS node. No retraining.

\medskip
\textbf{Where they are stored}

Inside the container:\\
ONNX: \file{/opt/models/pointpillars.onnx}\\
Engine: \file{/var/lib/pp_infer/<key>.engine}\\
Default persistent volume: \file{pp-engines}.

\medskip
\textbf{When an engine is built}

First startup uses \file{trtexec} to build an FP32 engine for the selected GPU.
Later starts reuse a matching engine. The cache identity includes the model,
GPU, driver and build settings.

\medskip
\textbf{What ROS loads}

\file{engine_path} selects the engine. TensorRT loads it once into memory and
prepares GPU buffers. Each cloud reuses it.

\medskip
\textbf{Keep the ONNX in the image:} startup still checks its checksum.
Direct \file{ros2 run} requires an already prepared engine.
\end{column}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}


\begin{frame}[t]{Measured KITTI accuracy remains poor}
\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
\begin{column}{.47\textwidth}
\centering\resizebox{.97\linewidth}{!}{\accuracyfigure}
\medskip
\small\raggedright
\textbf{Zero moderate 3D matches for all three classes.}
Vehicle is evaluated as KITTI Car compatibility. The 16 tuning and 16 test
frames are split by recording drive; this is a diagnostic sample.
\end{column}
\begin{column}{.50\textwidth}
\small\raggedright
\textbf{What the reference comparison found}
\begin{itemize}
\item TRT8 also reproduced poor accuracy and roughly 1.53 m upward car error.
\item Our NMS and NVIDIA's unchanged NMS selected identical boxes on
192/192 identical-candidate observations.
\item 12/32 clouds exceed 10,000 pillars. Two tuning-only controls improved
repeat matching to about 99.6--100\%; no production fix was installed.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Acceptance remains open.} Exact numerical/training parity, input
conventions, bounded voxelization and larger untouched accuracy evaluation
still need work. Two zero-error allocation probes do not establish memory safety.
\end{column}
\end{columns}
\medskip
\scriptsize Sources: \file{config/kitti_accuracy_inventory.json},
\file{config/kitti_reference_inventory.json}. Recorded 3 October 2026.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[t]{Later sensor experiments: numerical and integration results}
\small
\textbf{Ouster: only the fourth feature changed}
\medskip
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}Yrrrr@{}}
\toprule Feature & Rejected & Invalid rows & Median boxes & Score median \\
\midrule
Original signal & 15/48 & 33 & 590 & 1.000 \\
Signal / 65,535 & 0/48 & 0 & 15 & 0.164 \\
Reflectivity / 255 & 0/48 & 0 & 18.5 & 0.166 \\
min(reflectivity / 100, 1) & 0/48 & 0 & 26 & 0.173 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\medskip
\footnotesize
16 scans $\times$ 4 variants $\times$ 3 repeats = 192 observations.
All invalid rows had nonfinite length. Count medians use accepted observations;
score values are medians of per-observation medians. Fewer boxes do not prove accuracy.
\medskip
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Normalized Ouster replay:} 556/556 matched input/output occurrences;
no recorded detector log errors. Reflectivity/255 remains an opt-in diagnostic
encoding; the ordinary helper defaults to raw signal.
\item \textbf{Camera/detail:} 111 images and CameraInfo messages preserved;
header clocks differ. Original clouds are available on a separate topic.
\item \textbf{Frontal KITTI:} 154/154 matched clouds and detections, plus 154
images/CameraInfo; full scans retain 98,532--124,122 points. No ground truth in this clip.
\end{itemize}
\scriptsize Recorded 13--14 October. Sources: Ouster feature/camera/detail and
KITTI front inventories in \file{config/}. Detection accuracy remains unaccepted.
\end{frame}
